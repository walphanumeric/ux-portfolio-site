Career pages

Designed a self-service career site builder so customers could create and manage their own sites.

Problem

The largest competition to Snagajob job seeker traffic was company branded job pages.

Goals

  • Create a self-service web page builder for companies to post their jobs and brand their website
  • Replace the third-party vendor and the implementation team’s manual work, reducing cost for customers and Snagajob
  • Let customers update their own sites in real time and increase retention by offering the CMS for free

Details

Snagajob wanted a self-service content management system (CMS) that let customers, small and enterprise, create and manage their own career sites. Snagajob relied on a third-party vendor to build career sites for mid-level and enterprise clients, and its implementation team created and modified sites for lower-tier customers. Customers could use the sites to promote their job postings and build brand awareness, either as standalone sites or incorporated into an existing website. Moving this work in-house was intended to remove the vendor cost for customers and Snagajob, free the implementation team, let customers make changes in real time and increase customer satisfaction. Offering the CMS as a free product was also intended to increase customer retention.

Website Redesign

Redesigned the Snagajob website on a responsive, modern code structure.

Problem

Snagajob had an outdated website with an old development architecture, hindering user experience and development time.

Goals

  • Consolidate the desktop and mobile sites into one responsive site
  • Update the code structure to the modern Angular Material framework
  • Modernize the experience for job seekers and workers

Details

Apart from a recent re-skin, the site hadn’t been completely redesigned in several years. The code structure was becoming increasingly difficult to manage, and releases to production would periodically break midway through the process. The redesign took an iterative approach, with the first phase focused on the homepage, search results and job details pages, which made up the majority of site traffic.

Problem
