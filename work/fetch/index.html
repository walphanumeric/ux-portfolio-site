Problem

Fetch managed driver scheduling and payments in two separate third-party tools. Neither gave drivers or managers what they needed to do their jobs effectively.

Goals

  • Build a single native app for scheduling and pay, replacing third-party tools
  • Increase driver engagement and shift acceptance
  • Automate driver payroll and give drivers visibility into their pay

Details

This work covered two connected parts, scheduling and payments, with one goal: moving drivers and managers into a single app. The third-party scheduling app was slow, gave drivers inaccurate information about available shifts and made it easy to select the wrong warehouse by mistake. Payments were a manual process in a separate application, which gave drivers limited visibility into what they were paid and required managers to review driver payments every week. Manager research sessions and two rounds of driver usability testing shaped a native scheduling tool where drivers could select and manage their shifts and managers could manage schedules more easily. We also automated payroll and gave drivers more granular visibility into their payment history in the same app they used for shifts. Managers no longer needed to review weekly payments, saving an hour of work each week.

Apartment Maps

Problem

Fetch’s contract drivers had trouble navigating apartment properties to complete package deliveries, and driver support struggled to route them correctly or give accurate details.

Goals

  • Improve the completion of package deliveries
  • Resolve inaccurate building access information
  • Give drivers better complex layouts and in-complex unit mapping, and reduce outreach to driver support

Details

Contract drivers had trouble getting to apartment complexes, getting access to buildings and finding their way around inside complexes to finish deliveries. Driver support also had trouble routing drivers to the right destinations and giving accurate details. Updates to the map service aimed to fix inaccurate access information, provide better complex layouts so drivers could prepare for arrival, and add better in-complex unit mapping so drivers could complete deliveries accurately and contact driver support less often.
